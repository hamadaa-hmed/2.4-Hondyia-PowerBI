# 🚗 Hyundai Car Sales & Market Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Car Sales & Market Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes car sales data from **2018 to 2024**, providing insights into sales, profit, costs, transactions, customers, car models, car manufacturers, gender, and sales regions.

The goal of the project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 💰 Sales & Profit

- Total net sales reached approximately **459.2M**.
- Total profit reached approximately **45.6M**.
- Total cost was approximately **166.6M**.
- Overall profit margin remained around **9%–10%** across the analyzed years.

### 🚘 Car Manufacturers

The dashboard compares profitability across different car manufacturers.

Among the displayed manufacturers:

- Mercedes generated approximately **8M** in profit.
- BMW generated approximately **7M**.
- Audi generated approximately **7M**.
- Ford generated approximately **5M**.
- Other manufacturers included Chevrolet, Honda, Kia, Nissan, and Hyundai.

### 🔄 Transactions by Car Make

The dashboard also analyzes transaction volume by manufacturer.

Examples include:

- Ford: **306 transactions**
- Kia: **279**
- Nissan: **274**
- Honda: **270**
- BMW: **265**
- Mercedes: **248**
- Hyundai: **240**
- Toyota: **236**
- Audi: **225**
- Chevrolet: **220**

### 👥 Customer Analysis

The customer dashboard provides insights into:

- Total customers
- Customer sales
- Customer profit
- Customer gender
- Average transaction value
- Customer distribution over time

The overall customer base is approximately **2.5K customers**.

Male customers generated approximately **243.9M in sales** and **22.6M in profit** in the overall view.

### 🌍 Sales Regions

An interactive map was used to visualize the geographic distribution of sales transactions and customers.

### 📅 Yearly Analysis

The dashboard allows users to analyze performance from **2018 to 2024** using interactive year filters.

Users can also filter the analysis by months and other available dimensions.

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how Power BI can be used to:

1. Analyze large sales datasets.
2. Build interactive dashboards.
3. Track business KPIs.
4. Identify sales and profit trends.
5. Compare car manufacturers and models.
6. Analyze customer behavior.
7. Visualize geographic sales distribution.
8. Convert raw data into actionable business insights.
